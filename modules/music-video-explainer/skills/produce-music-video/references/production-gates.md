# Production gates

## Before generation

- Validate the fact pack and shot manifest.
- Confirm source assets and the master duration.
- Confirm the provider, mode, take count, and maximum spend.
- Test one rapid-consonant and one sustained-vowel performance line.

## Before full-length production

- Accept at least one take for every proof performance segment at normal playback speed.
- Reject identity drift, obscured mouths, unwanted cuts, and incorrect emotion.
- Verify every map label and metric against the cited source.
- Confirm the performer and graphics share palette, type, texture, and grade.
- Stop if two approved takes do not produce an acceptable result; ask before spending more or changing providers.

## Before delivery

- Probe the final MP4 for intended dimensions, frame rate, duration, and audio.
- Listen for generated-audio leakage and sync errors.
- Preserve the editable project and all machine-readable contracts.
- Record provider prediction IDs, artifact hashes, actual costs, retries, and any human interventions.
