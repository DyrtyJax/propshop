# Quality gates

## Story and editorial

- Every beat advances the thesis or earns its place emotionally.
- The opening creates a useful question; the ending resolves or redirects it.
- Narration, visuals, and interaction complement rather than repeat each other.
- The sequence has a comprehensible shape when viewed as still thumbnails.

## Visual system

- Review a representative hero frame at the actual output size.
- Type, icon, color, image treatment, spacing, and motion share one logic.
- Important text remains selectable or editable in source artifacts.
- Contrast, captions, alt text, reduced motion, and keyboard behavior suit the destination.

## Motion and interaction

- Motion explains change, hierarchy, causality, geography, or emotion.
- Deterministic renders seek to the same state at the same time.
- Interactive scenes have a readable initial state, focus behavior, and fallback.
- Expensive generation follows an approved short spike and rough sequence.

## Facts, maps, and assets

- Displayed claims resolve to verified fact records and sources.
- Maps record source, date, license, projection, precision, and attribution.
- Generated or licensed assets record origin and usage rights.
- Labels, legends, axes, units, and uncertainty survive small screens and exports.

## Destination QA

- Video: inspect representative frames, audio sync, captions, pacing, and final encode.
- Slides: inspect native editability, presenter flow, overflow, and exported PDF.
- Scroll: test narrow and wide screens, keyboard, reduced motion, and slow loading.
- Interactive: test first-use comprehension, reset/replay, touch, focus, and fallback.

Keep the portable story, editable project sources, provenance records, and final exports together. A rerender should not require reconstructing intent from chat logs.
